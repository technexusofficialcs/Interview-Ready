COLD EMAIL — INTERNSHIP & JOB OUTREACH
=========================================

1. WHAT IS A COLD EMAIL?
------------------------
A cold email means directly emailing a company, recruiter, or HR professional to express your interest in an internship or job opportunity.

It is called “cold” because you are contacting someone without having an existing conversation or connection with them.

Example:
You find a company that works in web development and email their HR directly asking about internship opportunities.


2. HOW TO FIND COMPANIES?
-------------------------
Search for companies related to your field using:

• LinkedIn — Search companies, recruiters, HR professionals, and employees.
• Internshala — Look for relevant internships and companies.
• Google — Search for companies, startups, agencies, and organizations working in your field.
• Company websites — Explore their careers, hiring, or contact pages.

Search examples:
• Web Development companies hiring interns
• Software development startups India
• React internship companies
• Full Stack Development internship
• Python developer internship

Tip:
Do not restrict yourself only to large companies. Startups and smaller companies can also provide valuable hands-on experience.


3. HOW TO FIND COMPANY EMAILS?
------------------------------
Once you identify a company, look for a professional contact or HR email.

Check:

1. Company website
2. Contact Us page
3. About Us page
4. Careers / Jobs page
5. LinkedIn company page
6. Google search

Look specifically for addresses such as:

• hr@company.com
• careers@company.com
• recruitment@company.com
• jobs@company.com

Important:
Prefer an official company email address and verify it before sending.


4. SUBJECT LINE TIPS
--------------------
Your subject line should immediately tell the recipient why you are contacting them.

Simple formula:

Application for [Role] Internship – [Your Name]

Example:

Application for Web Developer Internship – Anushka Chaturvedi

Other examples:

• Application for Full Stack Development Internship – Anushka Chaturvedi
• Application for Python Development Internship – Anushka Chaturvedi
• Internship Application – Computer Science Undergraduate

Avoid:

• Hi
• Job
• Internship
• Please read
• Urgent!!!

Keep it clear, specific, and professional.


5. ATTACH RESUME + PORTFOLIO
----------------------------
Always include your resume when applying through email.

You can also add:

• GitHub
• Portfolio website
• LinkedIn
• Relevant project/demo links

Recommended structure:

Resume: Attached PDF
GitHub: Your GitHub profile
LinkedIn: Your LinkedIn profile
Portfolio: If available

FILE NAMING

Instead of:
resume_final_final2.pdf

Use:
Anushka_Chaturvedi_Resume.pdf

This looks much more professional.


6. THE “100 EMAILS” STRATEGY
----------------------------
The basic idea is to increase your outreach volume instead of depending on a single application.

However, do not simply send the exact same email to 100 companies.

Better approach:

Create one strong base email → personalize a few important parts for each company.

Personalize:

• Company name
• Internship/job role
• One relevant skill
• One relevant project
• Why you are interested in that company/role

Example:

Instead of:
“I am interested in any internship opportunity at your company.”

Write:
“I am particularly interested in the Full Stack Development internship because of the opportunity to work with React, Node.js, and database-driven applications.”

This makes your outreach feel intentional rather than mass-generated.


7. WRITING AN EMAIL WITH CHATGPT
--------------------------------
You can use ChatGPT to create a professional email based on a specific internship/job description.

STEP 1 — FIND THE INTERNSHIP/JOB

Go to platforms such as:

• Internshala
• LinkedIn
• Company career pages
• Other job portals

Find your desired internship/job role.

STEP 2 — COPY THE JOB DESCRIPTION

Copy the complete job listing, starting from:

Role / Internship Title

and include the relevant information until the end of the listing.

This gives ChatGPT enough context about:

• Role
• Responsibilities
• Required skills
• Eligibility
• Duration
• Company
• Requirements
• Other relevant details

STEP 3 — PASTE IT INTO CHATGPT

Then ask:

“Draft a short professional email applying for this internship. Highlight my relevant skills and projects, keep it concise, and do not add any experience or skills that are not present in my resume.”

10. SENDING THE EMAIL
---------------------
Gmail workflow:

1. Open Gmail.
2. Click Compose.
3. Enter the verified company/HR email.
4. Add a professional subject.
5. Paste your email.
6. Attach your resume.
7. Add relevant links — GitHub | LinkedIn | Portfolio.
8. Proofread everything.
9. SEND.

Example:

To:
hr@company.com

Subject:
Application for Full Stack Development Internship – Anushka Chaturvedi


11. FINAL COLD EMAIL CHECKLIST
-----------------------------
Before clicking Send, check:

☐ Correct company name
☐ Correct internship/job title
☐ Verified recipient email
☐ Clear subject line
☐ Professional greeting
☐ Relevant skills mentioned
☐ No false claims
☐ Resume attached
☐ Resume has a professional filename
☐ GitHub/LinkedIn/portfolio added where relevant
☐ No spelling or grammatical errors
☐ No placeholder text such as [Company Name]
☐ Email is concise and easy to scan


12. SIMPLE CHATGPT PROMPT — FOR BEGINNERS
------------------------------------------
I want to apply for this internship. Read the job description below and draft a short, professional email to the hiring team. Mention only the skills and experience that are relevant to the role and that I actually have. Keep the email concise and natural. Do not invent any experience, skills, projects, achievements, or qualifications. Include a professional subject line.

Job Description:
[Paste the complete job description here]

My Skills/Experience:
[Paste your resume or relevant details here]


13. ADVANCED CHATGPT PROMPT
---------------------------
Act as a professional internship outreach assistant. Analyze the job description I provide and draft a concise cold email for the hiring team.

Your tasks:

1. Identify the most relevant requirements from the job description.
2. Match them only with skills, projects, and experience that I actually possess.
3. Write a clear professional subject line.
4. Write a short email with a strong opening.
5. Mention 2–4 highly relevant skills rather than listing everything I know.
6. Briefly connect my experience/projects to the role.
7. Mention that my resume is attached.
8. Include GitHub, LinkedIn, or portfolio only where relevant.
9. Keep the email concise and easy for a recruiter to scan.
10. Do not invent or exaggerate any experience, skills, achievements, metrics, or qualifications.

Job Description:
[Paste job description]

My Resume/Profile:
[Paste resume or relevant information]

Output:
• Subject line
• Final email
• 3–5 bullet points explaining why the selected skills/projects are relevant


KEY TAKEAWAY
------------
Cold email = Research → Find the right contact → Personalize → Attach resume → Send professionally → Follow up appropriately.

The goal is not simply to send more emails. It is to send more relevant, well-targeted emails without sacrificing professionalism or accuracy.
